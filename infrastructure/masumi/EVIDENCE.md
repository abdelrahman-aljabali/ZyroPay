### Sweeper CPU (wrangler tail, cron events)

| Build                                                     | CPU per sweep (ms)              | Outcome                   |

contain our 28-byte key hash (`datumMentionsKeyHash`, a necessary condition; the full parse
still decides). Wall time is dominated by Blockfrost round trips (0.3-1.9 s), which do not
count against the CPU limit.
