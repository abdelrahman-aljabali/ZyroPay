ZyroPay is a non-custodial wallet where your AI can ask to pay, and you decide. An AI assistant
(Claude, ChatGPT or any MCP client) connects to ZyroPay over OAuth. It can read balances and ask
for payments, but it holds no key and neither does the server it talks to. Every request is
checked against the policy you set for that agent, and by default you approve it with your
passkey on a review of exactly what will be signed. On Cardano, ZyroPay pays HTTP 402 (x402)
resources directly or into Masumi escrow, verifies the seller's signed delivery receipt, and
compares the result hash the seller records on chain with the bytes your agent actually
received.

## Design in five lines

<ol class="zp-five">
  <li>The agent asks; it never holds a key, and the MCP server it talks to cannot sign either.</li>
  <li>
    Your policy decides first: which tools, wallets, networks, recipients and protocols, what USD
    limits, escrow above a threshold, registered sellers only.
  </li>
  <li>
    Your passkey signs what you reviewed: the approval is bound to a SHA-256 of the payment's terms,
    and the API refuses any other bytes.
  </li>
  <li>
    On Cardano the payment is a plain x402 transfer, or a lock in Masumi escrow that the seller
    collects only after it records a result hash on chain.
  </li>
  <li>
    ZyroPay checks the delivery: the seller's signature over the exact bytes received, and their
    hash against the on-chain result, so you know when to ask for a refund.
  </li>
</ol>

## The stack

<Stack />

ZyroPay is the permission layer. MCP, x402, Masumi and Cardano are open technologies it uses;
Turnkey holds the keys in secure enclaves, behind your passkey.

## Who it is for

- **People who want their AI to buy things** such as data or API calls, without handing it a
  wallet, a seed phrase or a card.
- **Agent builders** who need a payment tool with a human in the loop, limits and an audit trail,
  over a standard MCP connector.
- **Sellers of paid APIs** on Cardano who want buyers that pay per request over x402 and can check
  what they received.

## Status

- **Test networks only.** Cardano preprod, Solana devnet, Ethereum Sepolia and Base Sepolia, at
  https://app.zyropay.online. Production is not deployed.
- **Not audited.** No external security review has taken place. The threat model and its open
  items are in [Security and trust](/security/).
- **On Cardano, the passkey or an on-chain allowance.** Turnkey cannot bound a Cardano signature
  with a policy, so an agent pays on Cardano only with your passkey approval, or with no tap from
  an allowance you created with your passkey, whose limits the Cardano ledger enforces. See
  [Autonomous mode](/how-it-works/autonomous/).
- Everything that is not yet proven live is listed in [Limits and roadmap](/limits/).
