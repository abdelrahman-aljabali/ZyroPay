## Not audited, not on mainnet

- **No security audit** and no threat-model sign-off. Both block a launch.
- **Test networks only.** Production is not deployed: no mainnet secrets, no production database, no
  production domains.
- **No payment privacy.** Payer, payee, amounts and times are public on Cardano, and an enterprise
  address carries its whole history.

## Known gaps in the security model

- **The device checks outputs, not inputs.** On Cardano the app decodes the transaction itself and
  refuses to sign if any output, the fee, the validity window or the escrow datum differs from what
  it shows <Status kind="offline" />. It cannot see which UTxOs the inputs are, or recompute the
  `script_data_hash` (that needs the protocol cost model); those stay with the API. For a refund
  withdrawal it proves everything goes back to you, but not the exact amount.
- **The proof trusts the MCP worker's measurement** of the body your agent received.
- **Registry identity is checked at quote time,** not again at approval.
- **Blockfrost is the only view of the chain** for confirmations and escrow state.
- **The Masumi admins** (2 of 3) can settle a disputed escrow after the dispute time.
- **Turnkey is the custody root of trust.** ZyroPay verifies the signatures it returns, which detects
  a wrong signature, not a malicious one.

## Not yet proven live <Status kind="pending" />

Built and tested offline, waiting for a run on preprod:

- a refund withdrawn after the deadline without the seller's authorization (`not_delivered`; the
  `mismatch` refund with the seller's authorization is [live](/evidence/#dishonest-seller-caught-refund-on-chain));
- push notifications (staging has none; the app polls);
- the native iOS and Android apps on a device (the web app is live);
- wallet import against a real Turnkey org.

## Auto-pay on Cardano <Status kind="live" />

- **Live from the app on preprod, not audited.** One create and one no-tap x402 payment by ChatGPT
  ran on 2026-10-31 ([Evidence](/evidence/#auto-pay-the-agent-paid-with-no-tap-limits-enforced-by-cardano)).
  Top-up, revoke and the fallbacks are tested offline only <Status kind="offline" />. Preprod only.
- **The agent key is held by ZyroPay's server**: one Ed25519 key per allowance, encrypted at rest
  (AES-256-GCM), destroyed on revoke. The chain bounds what it can do (per payment, per day, listed
  payees, expiry, plus its fee float); it cannot touch the rest of your wallet.
- **One payment at a time per allowance**: the next one waits for the previous to confirm (about
  20 s), and meanwhile falls back to the passkey (`ALLOWANCE_BUSY`).
- **Direct ADA payments only**: tokens, swaps and Masumi escrow keep the passkey.
- **Fiat limits are converted once, at creation**; the chain counts lovelace.

## Not built <Status kind="notbuilt" />

- **x402 delivery on EVM and Solana.** Payments are signed but not handed to the resource.
- **Concurrent Cardano sends from one address.** Without UTxO reservation the second one fails with
  `inputs_spent`; retry it.
- **Pre-registered OAuth clients** (needed if a client such as Grok wants a manually entered client
  id).
- **The 2026-07-28 MCP transport.** The server speaks the stateless 2025-11-25 transport, which
  overlaps; the upgrade path is documented in the MCP reference.

## Measured, not optimized

Every live payment from ZyroPay so far was in ADA. The one native-token payment is the Sokosumi Task
(1 tUSDM), paid by Sokosumi into the escrow and collected by our Payment Service.

- A direct x402 payment took about 160 s from request to `CONFIRMED` and the escrow lock about 130 s,
  most of it the human approval. The seller's SubmitResult landed 21 s after the lock.
- Fees: 0.167349 ADA for a plain payment, 0.187589 ADA for an escrow lock (with the inline datum),
  0.688128 ADA for the seller's SubmitResult and 0.642366 ADA for its Withdraw (a 10 KB Plutus
  script inline in each). A reference script would make seller spends cheaper; not done.
- The seller's Withdraw landed 39 s after `unlockTime`, inside one cron period.
- The refund: 0.695215 ADA for the passkey's SetRefundRequested and 0.642818 ADA for its
  WithdrawRefund (paid out of the escrow); 4 min 54 s from the lock to the money back, of which the
  seller's AuthorizeRefund took 32 s after the request.
- An agent payment from the on-chain allowance cost 0.291123 ADA (the 1.9 KB script inline, units
  measured by the evaluator and declared exactly); the owner's revoke 0.259217 ADA. In the app,
  the allowance create cost 0.174037 ADA and the auto-paid x402 payment 0.293462 ADA, paid from the
  agent's float, not from the allowance.
