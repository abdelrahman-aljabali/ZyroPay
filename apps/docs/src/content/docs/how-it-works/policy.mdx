Every agent request goes through one pure function, `evaluatePolicy` in `@zyropay/shared`, inside a
database transaction that holds a row lock on the agent's policy. It returns one of three decisions
and every reason that applied: **deny**, **require approval** (ask you), or **allow** (autonomous,
within limits). Read-only tools are allowed once the agent, tool, wallet and network checks pass;
approval applies to value only.

## The rules, in order

1. **Who and where.** Deny if the agent is revoked or its session expired, or if the tool, the
   wallet or the network is not in the agent's allowed lists.
2. **What and to whom.** For a value-moving tool, deny if spending is off; if the protocol is
   missing or not allowed (`pay_x402` must name `x402`); or if recipients are restricted and the
   recipient is not on the allowlist. For an x402 payment the recipient is the seller, also for an
   escrow payment where the money goes to a script first. With **only pay registered Masumi
   agents** on, deny a seller without an on-chain identity ZyroPay verified (`AGENT_NOT_REGISTERED`)
   or, if you keep a trusted list, one not on it (`AGENT_NOT_TRUSTED`).
3. **How much.** The request is valued in USD cents, rounded up. If the value plus what was already
   spent **and reserved** today or this month exceeds a limit, or there is no valuation, the request
   is over the limit. So is a direct (non-escrow) x402 payment above your escrow threshold
   (`ESCROW_REQUIRED`). Over the limit means deny or ask you, as you choose (`overLimitBehavior`).
4. **Who signs.** In Always Ask mode, for a swap, and for any Cardano payment, ask you. Otherwise
   allow: the payment runs autonomously within the limits.

A request that is allowed or sent to you for approval reserves its USD value at once; the
reservation becomes a ledger entry when the chain confirms the payment, and is released if it is
rejected, expires or fails.

## Why it fails closed

- **No price, no autonomy.** USD values come from Jupiter (Solana) and CoinGecko (Ethereum, Base,
  ADA). A quote older than 120 s is dropped. A missing valuation counts as exceeding every limit, so
  the request is denied or comes to you; it is never allowed.
- **Test networks are valued at mainnet prices** and labelled `mainnet_proxy`, so limits mean the
  same thing on preprod as they would on mainnet.
- **A throw is a deny.** Malformed amounts throw, and the caller treats a throw as a denial.
- **Concurrency.** Each agent's requests are serialized by `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` on its policy row,
  and limits count spent plus reserved. Ten requests fired at once cannot jointly pass a daily limit
  that one of them alone would fit. This is tested on an in-process Postgres and on real Postgres.

## What a new agent may do

Nothing that moves value. `defaultAgentPolicy()` is Always Ask, spending off, zero limits, recipients
restricted to an empty allowlist, no wallets, no protocols. Connecting the agent adds only the
wallets and networks you picked; everything else you switch on yourself in the policy editor. Every
field is in the [policy reference](/reference/policy/), every reason code in
[States and codes](/reference/codes/#policy-reason-codes).

## A worked example

The live x402 payment was 2 tADA from ChatGPT to the demo seller. For it to get past rules 1 to 3,
the agent's policy had spending on, the `x402` protocol allowed and the seller's address on the
allowlist (the setup in the [user guide](/guides/users/#set-the-agents-policy)). Rule 4 then asked
for approval, as it does for every Cardano payment, and the request came back `PENDING_APPROVAL`.
The same agent asking to pay an address that is not on the allowlist gets `REJECTED` with
`RECIPIENT_NOT_ALLOWED`, and nothing reaches the app or the chain. That ran live on 2026-10-31:
ChatGPT asked to send 1 tADA to an address outside the allowlist, and ZyroPay rejected the request
and a retry before either reached the phone. After the user added the address to the allowlist in
the app, the same request went to the phone, was approved with the passkey and was broadcast
(<TxRef id="policy-allowlist" />).
